An offline browser app that turns up to three of your photos and a caption into GTA 6 style cover art. It paints a multi panel poster with a Vice City neon sunset grade and exports the cover as a PNG to share. No photo ever leaves your machine.

## What it does

Four layouts: Vice Grid, Nine Panel, Full Bleed and Tilted Snaps. Five colour grades in the neon sunset family: Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Dusk, Heat Wave and Chrome Blue. Each photo panel has its own zoom and drag to pan, and panels without a photo show art drawn from code: a striped retro sun, a lit skyline, palm silhouettes, a sports car over a neon grid, and halftone dots. The title is set in Anton and the subtitle in Pacifico, both bundled under app/fonts with their SIL Open Font License texts. Export sizes are 1080 by 1080, 1080 by 1350 and 1920 by 1080.

## Run it

Node 22 or later, nothing to install:

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Load a photo from your machine or use a sample photo, pick a layout and a grade, write the caption, drag a panel to move its photo, and press Export PNG.
